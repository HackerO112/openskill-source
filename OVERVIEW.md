# OpenSkill

A free, open, Skillshare-style learning site for anyone. Learners pick any skill — from programming to knitting to car repair — and get a structured "zero to master" course built from the best teachers on YouTube. Courses are assembled automatically: the site searches YouTube, ranks videos by views, likes and creator reputation, and AI orders the clearest lessons into Beginner → Master levels.

No accounts: progress, notes and streaks are saved in each visitor's browser, with export/import to move between devices. A directory of 1,000+ skills across 22 fields means every skill can be learned; courses build on first open and are reused for everyone after.
